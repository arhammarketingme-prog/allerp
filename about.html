About Us (आमच्याबद्दल)

ALL ERP & Aapla Bazaar

Aapla Bazaar (ALL ERP) is a local business technology platform built to help small and medium retailers, wholesalers, and service businesses run their shops digitally — inventory, billing, a local online storefront, delivery, customer ledgers (Khata), and more — all from one place, without needing to hire a developer.

What We Do

  • Give every registered local shop a free online storefront where nearby customers can browse and order
  • Provide a complete point-of-sale, inventory, and billing system for the shop's own daily operations
  • Run local last-mile delivery, either through the shop's own delivery staff or our delivery partner pool
  • Offer a Customer Khata (credit ledger) tool for shops that extend credit to regular customers
  • Connect retailers directly with registered wholesalers for bulk purchasing
  • Run Community Group Buying pools so nearby customers can pool demand for better factory-direct pricing
  • Provide advertising space for local businesses and brands who want to reach shoppers in their area

What We Don't Do

ALL ERP is a technology platform, not a seller. We do not hold or take custody of payments for products sold through the platform — those transactions happen directly between the customer and the shop. We do not take a commission or revenue share from product sales.
